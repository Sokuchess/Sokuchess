Arc Spin

Spin falling arcs so the ball slips through. Calm music, changing scenes, and a sky that moves from day to night.

How to play
Free · works best on a phone · turn the sound on

How to play

The ball never moves. You rotate the arcs.

1

Swipe to rotate

Slide your finger left or right anywhere on the screen to spin the lowest arc. On a computer, use the arrow keys.

2

Open it over the ball

Turn the arc so its gap faces the ball. When the arc's centre drops below the ball, flip the gap the other way.

3

Don't touch the line

The empty space inside an arc is safe. Touch the outline and it's game over. Every arc you pass scores a point.

What's inside

Built to feel relaxing, with a bit of challenge.

Day and night

The sky slowly shifts through day, sunset, night and dawn, with stars and drifting clouds.

Calming music

Music is generated live and changes mood with the time of day. No downloads.

Angle meter

A dial and a sliding ruler show the arc's angle. Ticks sound every 15° and the pitch rises as you turn.

Gifts and celebrations

Every 5 arcs a gift lands with confetti, and every 25 arcs the fireworks start.

Four scenes

The place changes about once a minute, and each place has its own day and night.

Lake & mountains

Still water and layered peaks.

Desert

Dunes, mesas and cacti.

Beach

Waves, sand and palm trees.

Forest

Misty pines and quiet ground.
